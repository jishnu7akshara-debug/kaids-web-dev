# Responsive & iOS Safari Alignment Audit

Environment verified via Playwright (Chromium headless across 14 viewports, 320 px–2560 px) + CSS/JS static inspection. *(Real iOS device / Xcode Simulator not available on this Windows host; iOS-specific fixes follow Apple WebKit / Safari layout specifications and are flagged for final on-device check.)*

| Viewport | Section | Issue | Root Cause | Fix | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 320–834 px (touch) | Services Ticker | Auto-scrolling continuously on phones | `site.js` cloned and animated all `[data-marquee]` unconditionally | Gated `.ticker .marquee` animation on `matchMedia('(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine) and (min-width: 62rem)')` with dynamic `change` listener; static `scroll-snap-type: x proximity` row on touch | Fixed |
| All touch viewports | Global / Ticker / Cards | Tap-highlight flash & long-press callout ("lit up" artefact) | Missing `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent`, missing `user-select: none` on ticker/marquees/cards, `.skip:focus` rule | Added `-webkit-tap-highlight-color: transparent` on `html`, `:active` opacity feedback, `:focus-visible` everywhere, and `user-select: none; -webkit-touch-callout: none` on `.ticker`, `.marquee`, `.partner-card` | Fixed |
| Mobile / iOS | Ticker & Marquees | Edge `mask-image` repaint flicker & font-load width jump | `mask-image` on `.marquee-view`, 2D `translateX(-50%)`, no `IntersectionObserver` pause or `document.fonts.ready` recalc | Replaced `mask-image` with gradient pseudo-elements (`::before`/`::after`), switched to `translate3d(-50%, 0, 0)` + `will-change: transform` + `backface-visibility: hidden`, paused off-screen marquees, recalculated duration on `fonts.ready` and debounced `resize` | Fixed |
| All viewports | Partners | Role lines shown under partner names | `.partner-role` in `index.html`, `partners.html`, dialogs, `jobTitle` in JSON-LD, `alt` text, and `partners-og.jpg` | Removed role lines across cards, dialogs, `/partners`, JSON-LD `jobTitle`, `partners.json`, `alt` attributes, and regenerated `partners-og.jpg` | Fixed |
| All viewports | Global `.wrap` / Safe areas | Missing `viewport-fit=cover` and `env(safe-area-inset-*)` | Viewport meta lacked `viewport-fit=cover`; `.wrap` used `padding-inline: var(--gutter)` only | Added `viewport-fit=cover` to all HTML pages and `max(var(--gutter), env(safe-area-inset-*))` on `.wrap` and marquees | Fixed |
| Mobile / Modal | Bio Dialog (`100vh`) | `100vh` clips or jumps when iOS Safari address bar collapses | `.partner-dialog` and `.dialog-panel` used `100vh` without `100svh` | Added `100svh` after `100vh` fallback on `.partner-dialog` and `.dialog-panel` | Fixed |
| 320–834 px (touch) | Links, Chips, Footer | Touch targets below 44 × 44 px (`.chip`, `.text-link`, `.site-footer a`) | Inline links and chips had natural text height (16–27 px) on touch devices | Added `@media (hover: none), (pointer: coarse)` rule setting `min-height: 2.75rem` (`44px`) on `.chip`, `.site-footer a`, and standalone `.text-link`s | Fixed |
| 320–834 px | Footer | Bottom `.mobile-bar` could overlap last footer line | Fixed `padding-bottom: 6rem` without `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` | Updated `.site-footer` mobile `padding-bottom: calc(5.5rem + env(safe-area-inset-bottom, 0px))`; stacked `.footer-legal` cleanly with `0.35rem` gap | Fixed |
| All viewports | Footer Credit | KAIDS copyright instead of Verna Developers + Digital Footprint | Legacy copyright line in all 11 HTML pages | Replaced with `© 2026 Verna Developers. All rights reserved.` and `Developed by Digital Footprint` (`https://www.digifootprint.in/`, `target="_blank" rel="noopener"`) | Fixed |
| 320–1440 px | Contact `.desk-grid` & `.desk-card` | Cards used `border-radius: 4px`, undefined `--paper-warm` background, `margin-top: 2rem` fighting `.section-head`, lacked `minmax(0, 1fr)` on phones, and jumped to 3 cols at `48rem` | Updated `.desk-grid` (`minmax(0, 1fr)` mobile, `2 cols` @ `40rem`, `3 cols` @ `62rem`), `.desk-card` square hairline (`border-radius: 0; background: var(--white); border: 1px solid var(--line); padding: clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 1.75rem)`), sentence-case headings ("specialised", "and"), `aria-label` + `min-height: 2.75rem` on `.desk-call` | Fixed |
| 320–834 px | Contact `.phones`, `.form`, `.mobile-bar` | Last `.phones a` kept bottom hairline; `.contact-grid` went 2-col at `56rem`; `.mobile-bar` only checked `#contact` (not `#contact-form` on `contact.html`) | Removed `:last-child` border on `.phones a`, moved `.contact-grid` 2-col breakpoint to `62rem`, full-width submit button on `< 40rem`, `1.5rem` checkbox, and updated `site.js` to check `#contact, #contact-form` | Fixed |
