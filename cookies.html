Cookie disclosure

The public website for Karnataka Industrial Documentation Services sets zero cookies and deploys no third-party tracking scripts.

Public Website: Zero Cookies

When you browse our public homepage, services list, or client directory, no cookies are placed on your device. We do not use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or behavioral tracking identifiers. Because we use no non-essential cookies, no intrusive consent banner is required on public pages.
