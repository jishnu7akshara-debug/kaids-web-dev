Privacy notice

This notice explains how Karnataka Industrial Documentation Services ("KAIDS") collects, uses, protects, and retains your personal data in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) and the Information Technology Act, 2000.

1. Data Fiduciary Identity

Karnataka Industrial Documentation Services ("KAIDS") is the Data Fiduciary responsible for your personal data collected via this website.

2. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to evaluate and respond to your industrial documentation and liaison enquiry:

  • Contact & Identification: Your name, phone number, email address (optional), and company name (optional).
  • Enquiry Details: The service category selected and any message or project background provided.
  • Technical Security Data: A salted cryptographic hash of your network IP address (we never store raw IP addresses) and browser family, collected strictly for abuse prevention and rate limiting.

What we never collect: We set no tracking cookies, use no advertising trackers, execute no cross-site profiling, and deploy no third-party marketing beacons on public pages.

3. Purpose and Legal Basis

Purpose: To review your project requirements, communicate with you regarding statutory KIADB filings or industrial liaison services, and prepare service proposals.
Legal Basis: Your explicit consent, provided when you check the consent box on our enquiry form prior to submission.

4. Data Processors & Hosting Locations

We partner with reputable infrastructure providers acting strictly as Data Processors under contractual data protection terms:

  • Hosting: Vercel Inc. (Cloud serverless hosting, edge distribution).
  • Database: Neon, Inc. (Serverless PostgreSQL database, hosted in secure AWS cloud regions).
  • Notification Email: Resend, Inc. (Transactional email delivery to our Bengaluru office inboxes).

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data under any circumstances.

5. Data Retention & Erasure

Active client enquiries are retained for up to 24 months following closure, after which personal identifiers (name, phone, email, message) are permanently anonymized or purged by automated scheduled retention jobs. Security audit logs are maintained for a minimum of 13 months pursuant to DPDP regulatory standards.

6. Your Rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access: Obtain a summary of personal data held about you and processing activities undertaken.
  • Correction & Completion: Request correction of inaccurate, misleading, or incomplete data.
  • Erasure & Withdrawal: Withdraw your consent at any time and request erasure of your data.
  • Nomination: Nominate an individual to exercise your data principal rights in the event of incapacity.
  • Grievance Redressal: Raise inquiries or complaints with our designated Grievance Officer.

8. Security Safeguards

KAIDS implements industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards including Argon2id cryptographic password hashing, strict Role-Based Access Control, two-factor authentication for administrative staff, automated rate limiting, salted IP hashing, and encrypted data transmission (TLS 1.3).

9. Children's Data

Our website and liaison services are directed exclusively to business enterprises and adult corporate representatives. We do not knowingly solicit or collect information from individuals under the age of 18.
