About KAIDS

KAIDS began in 2015 with one observation: Bengaluru's industries had nobody dependable to run their paperwork with KIADB and the departments around it.

Our story

Gurunath, Tejas and Ajay founded Karnataka Industrial Documentation Services to bridge that gap. What started as direct liaison work on KIADB files has evolved into a complete industrial documentation, statutory compliance and project execution practice.

Experience and expertise

Over a decade of on-ground experience across Karnataka's key industrial hubs. Our team handles complex documentation, regulatory approvals, and project management—from initial allotment to turnkey industrial shed construction.

Our motivation

Every hour an industrialist spends following up on files is an hour taken away from business growth. We assume the full administrative burden so clients can focus on expanding production with complete peace of mind.

How a file moves.

A structured, dependable engagement workflow whether you need a single statutory NOC or end-to-end industrial commissioning.

  1. 01. Consult & Feasibility

    Tell us the plot, survey number, and proposed activity. We review KIADB zoning and define the complete statutory permission roadmap.

  2. 02. Document & Check

    We assemble project reports, check architectural layouts against building bylaws, and audit every paper to prevent departmental queries.

  3. 03. Liaise & Follow Up

    We submit files across official desks and online portals, attend technical queries, coordinate inspections, and follow up through sanction.

  4. 04. Handover & Archival

    You receive sanctioned building drawings, official orders, and statutory NOCs, bound with an archival audit record for your corporate records.
