Partners

Three partners, one family firm, two decades of Bengaluru land and industrial work between KAIDS and Verna Developers.

Gurunath/Tejas/Ajay

Gurunath D Vernekar

Founder and managing partner

Has worked with Bengaluru's land and industrial authorities since 2000.

Gurunath founded Verna Developers in 2000 and has advised on commercial, industrial and agricultural property in and around Bengaluru ever since. Seeing how often industrial owners struggled with KIADB paperwork, he co-founded KAIDS with Tejas Vernekar in 2015 to run that work end to end. He leads the firm's client relationships and its reading of the market.

Focus areas

  • Market analysis
  • KIADB and land matters
  • Client advisory
