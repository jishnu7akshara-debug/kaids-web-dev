Complete Industrial Scope

Comprehensive services, one point of contact.

From land allotment and statutory clearances to turnkey industrial shed construction, we carry the file across every department.

Land and KIADB, three services

Land and KIADB 3 services

  • Application Assistance

    Applications to KIADB and allied departments, prepared and filed complete.

  • Land Allotment Documentation

    The full paper trail for an industrial plot, from application through allotment.

  • Dispute Solutions & Ownership Transfer

    Support in resolving contested KIADB matters, arbitration, and ownership transfer documentation.

Approvals and clearances 3 services

  • Building Plan Sanction and Approvals

    Plan submissions followed through until the sanction is in hand.

  • NOC and Approvals (PCB and Fire)

    No-objection certificates from the Pollution Control Board and the Fire department.

  • Environmental Clearances & Compliance

    Applications for environmental clearances and ongoing statutory filings to keep units compliant.

Construction 3 services

  • Warehouse & Industrial Shed Construction

    Sheds, warehouses and industrial buildings, built from planning to execution.

  • Boundary Compound Wall Construction

    Boundary wall fencing and perimeter civil construction for industrial plots.

  • Building Demolition & Metal Scrap Purchase

    Demolition of existing structures, with purchase of the recovered metal scrap.

Plant and installations 3 services

  • Sewage and Effluent Treatment Plant

    STP and ETP installation for industrial units.

  • Fire Rescue Installation

    Fire safety and rescue systems installed on site.

  • Solar Panel Installation

    Solar panels installed on industrial premises.

Finance and legal 3 services

  • Loan Sanction

    Documentation and coordination to get project loans sanctioned.

  • Legal Documentation

    Agreements, deeds and legal paperwork for industrial property.

  • Project Report Preparation

    Project reports prepared for lenders and departments.
