Complete Industrial Scope

Comprehensive services, one point of contact.

From land allotment and statutory clearances to turnkey industrial shed construction, we carry the file across every department.

Land and KIADB

3 services

Authority: Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board (KIADB) • Deliverables: Allotment Letter, Lease-cum-Sale Agreement, Possession Certificate

  • Application Assistance

    Applications to KIADB and allied departments, prepared and filed complete.

  • Land Allotment Documentation

    The full paper trail for an industrial plot, from initial application through final allotment.

  • Dispute Solutions & Ownership Transfer

    Support in resolving contested KIADB matters, arbitration, and legal ownership transfer documentation.

Approvals and clearances

3 services

Authorities: KSPCB, Karnataka Fire & Emergency Services, Local Planning Bodies • Deliverables: Plan Sanction, CFE/CFO, Fire NOC

  • Building Plan Sanction and Approvals

    Plan submissions followed through official planning desks until the sanction order is in hand.

  • NOC and Approvals (PCB and Fire)

    No-objection certificates from the Pollution Control Board (CFE/CFO) and the Fire department.

  • Environmental Clearances & Compliance

    Applications for environmental clearances and ongoing statutory filings to keep manufacturing units compliant.

Construction

3 services

Compliance: Factories Act Bylaws • Deliverables: Turnkey PEB warehouse shed, boundary fencing, structural handover

  • Warehouse & Industrial Shed Construction

    Sheds, warehouses, and industrial buildings, built from engineering planning to site execution.

  • Boundary Compound Wall Construction

    Boundary wall fencing and perimeter civil construction securing industrial plots against encroachment.

  • Building Demolition & Metal Scrap Purchase

    Safe demolition of existing structures with competitive buyback of recovered structural steel and scrap.

Plant and installations

  • Sewage and Effluent Treatment Plant
