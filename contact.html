What to Bring

Documents for immediate file evaluation.

Having these on hand during your visit or call helps us diagnose approval roadmaps on day one.

Land & Allotment Papers

Provisional allotment letter, survey number sketch, lease-cum-sale agreement, or confirmation memo issued by KIADB.

Architectural & Site Drawings

Existing CAD layout drawings, site boundaries, proposed setbacks, and floor area ratios for building sanction evaluation.

Statutory Clearance Details

Manufacturing process summary, connected electrical load requirements (KVA), water consumption balance, and emission specs.

Turnkey Construction Scope

Intended shed eave height, soil test reports (if available), floor load parameters, and required commissioning date.
